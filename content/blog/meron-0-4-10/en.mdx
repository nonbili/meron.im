<PostSection>

An invoice arrives. A rule adds invoice and todo tags, and the thread appears in your To do column on the Kanban board while staying in your Inbox. When it’s ready for approval, drag it to Review. Meron swaps the todo tag for review, and you can see what needs attention at a glance. Here’s how to set up that workflow in Meron 0.4.10.

<PlatformNote>Tags, rules, and attachment search are available in desktop 0.4.10 and iOS and Android 0.4.8. MCP tag tools are available on desktop.</PlatformNote>

</PostSection>

## Give each thread a next step

Start by adding todo and invoice tags from the conversation header or thread menu. Tags sync to your mail server as standard IMAP keywords so they follow your messages across devices. They’re also preserved in Local Folders and exported backups.

<BlogImage src="/screenshots/blog/meron-0410-kanban-tags.png" alt="Meron Kanban board showing Inbox alongside tag:todo and tag:review columns with colored tag chips on thread cards and an unread badge on the board icon in the side rail" caption="Inbox, To do, and Review together on one board." width={1280} height={820} />

- **Drag to tag and retag on your board:** Save tag:todo and tag:review searches as Kanban columns. Drag a card from Inbox to add a tag, or between those columns to replace one workflow tag with the other. Other tags, such as invoice, stay on the thread.
- **Toggle tags in place:** Use the Tags submenu to toggle several tags without reopening it each time.
- **Rename or delete a tag everywhere:** Under Settings → Messages → Tags, rename or remove a tag across all mail stored on your device, updating your rules and Kanban board columns at the same time.

<BlogImage src="/screenshots/blog/meron-0410-tag-menu.png" alt="Thread context menu with the Tags flyout open showing checked tags and an Add a tag action" caption="Toggle multiple tags directly from the thread menu, or click any tag chip on a card to filter by that tag." width={1280} height={820} />

## Let the next invoice sort itself

To automate the first step, create a rule that matches a subject containing “invoice” and adds the invoice and todo tags. It runs on new Inbox messages, so the next matching invoice appears on your board ready to handle.

Open **Settings → Rules** to choose the accounts, conditions, and actions. Match by sender, recipient, subject, keywords, or attachments. Empty conditions match all new mail.

<BlogImage src="/screenshots/blog/meron-0410-rules.png" alt="Meron Settings showing the Rules list with drag handles, account avatars, rule summaries, Run buttons, and Recent activity log" caption="Run a rule on existing Inbox mail, then check Recent activity to see what ran." width={1280} height={820} />

- **Combine actions in one rule:** Alongside tags, a rule can mark mail as read, star it, copy it, or move it to another folder—even across accounts or into Local Folders on desktop.
- **Drag to reorder priority:** Rules run from top to bottom. The first rule that moves a message decides its destination. Reorder rules with their drag handles or the Up and Down arrow keys.
- **Run on existing mail &amp; check activity:** Click the Run button on any rule to apply it to messages already in your Inbox, and check Recent activity below your rules to see what ran and when.

## Find the mail behind your workflow

Search for tag:invoice has:attachment to find every invoice with an attachment, regardless of its workflow stage. Add sender or unread filters to narrow the results further.

- **Tag suggestions and attachment filter:** Search Options includes tag suggestions and a Has attachment checkbox if you prefer to build a filter with a form. Save the resulting search as a live Kanban column to keep it within reach.
- **Tag tools for MCP clients:** On desktop, local MCP clients with message organization access can call list\_tags and tag\_messages to inspect tags in use and add or remove tags on threads.
