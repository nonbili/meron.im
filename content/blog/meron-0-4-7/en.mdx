Unread mail from key clients. Invoices from the past month. Starred threads that need a reply. With saved search columns, you can give each of these a place on your Kanban board. Save a search once, and the column stays up to date as your mail changes.

## Turn a search into a live column

A folder gives a thread one place to live. Saved searches give you more ways to see it: by sender, date, read status, or a combination that fits your workflow.

Start with a search using familiar **Gmail-style operators** or the visual search builder, then choose *Save search as column*. Matching threads appear in a live column, with an editable query beneath its header.

- **Build a search without typing operators:**  Open the search options to filter by sender, recipient, subject, keywords, date range, or target column.
- **Narrow your board without losing your saved searches:**  The board-wide search adds a filter to each saved search column, keeping its original query in place.

## A local-first calendar and interactive invite cards

Calendar invitations now appear as cards inside the conversation, with the event time in your local timezone, location, organizer, and attendee responses. You can act on an invitation without leaving the thread.

<BlogImage src="/screenshots/blog/meron-047-invite-card.png" alt="Calendar invite card rendered inside the conversation view with Accept, Maybe, and Decline buttons" caption="See event details and respond directly inside the conversation." width={1280} height={820} />

- **Accept, Maybe, or Decline in place:**  Your response is sent to the organizer and recorded in Meron.
- **Automatic organizer updates:**  Meron applies incoming schedule changes and cancellations, including updates to individual recurring meetings.
- **Monthly local calendar:**  Enable Calendar in Settings to see personal events, accepted invitations, and task due dates together. Create all-day, timed, or repeating events, and jump from an invited event back to its email thread.

Your calendar is stored on your device and included in Meron's encrypted backups.

## Cross-account moves and recent folder shortcuts

Need to file a thread in another account? You can now **move threads across different email accounts** on both desktop and mobile—either by dragging a card between account columns on the Kanban board or choosing *Move to…* from the thread menu.

Meron copies the messages to the destination and verifies the transfer before moving the originals to Trash.

The *Move to* and *Copy to* menus now surface **recent destination shortcuts** at the top, making your usual folders quicker to reach.

## Save and copy inline images, plus desktop refinements

Desktop also gets a few everyday improvements:

- **Save &amp; copy inline images:**  Right-click an image in a message or open it in the media gallery to save it or copy it to your clipboard.
- **Cleaner menus and overlays:**  Menus and dialogs have more consistent keyboard focus and dismissal, with clearer submenu highlights and less flicker.
- **Clearer errors and more translations:**  Move and copy failures include more detail about the cause. Mobile also gets expanded translations across its 19 supported languages.
