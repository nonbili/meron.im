A full mailbox shouldn’t mean deleting mail you want to keep. Meron 0.4.8 adds Local Folders on desktop so you can move receipts, finished projects, and old conversations onto your computer. A new calendar side panel keeps upcoming work in view while you read.

</PostSection>

<Badge icon="HardDrive">Local Folders</Badge>

## Keep mail on your computer, off the server

Free up space in your IMAP mailbox or save your mail before closing an account. Local Folders give you an offline archive on your computer that you can browse and search in Meron.

Add **Local Folders** under Settings → Local folders. It starts with an Archive folder and appears alongside your mail accounts in the navigation rail, Kanban board, and search. Mail stored there stays on that computer.

- **Move or copy from any account:** Drag cards onto a local folder column on your Kanban board, or use Move to… and Copy to… from any thread menu. Moving a thread verifies that every message is safely stored on disk before moving the server copies to Trash.
- **Organize your local archive:** Create, rename, and nest folders inside Local Folders to structure your offline archive however you like.
- **Passphrase-encrypted archive backups:** Once you remove the server copies, your local archive may be the only copy of that mail. Use Settings → Local folders to export a backup and keep it somewhere separate from your computer. The exported backup is encrypted with a passphrase and can be restored in Meron on another computer.

## Put old mail where you can find it

Create a folder for each project, client, or year without leaving Meron. Desktop 0.4.8 supports folder creation and renaming on your IMAP server and in Local Folders; iOS and Android 0.4.7 bring the same controls to server folders.

- **Create folders anywhere:** Create folders and nested subfolders from mailbox and Kanban column menus.
- **Rename folders in place:** Kanban columns and recent transfer shortcuts follow the new folder name.
- **Find a destination quickly:** Move to… and Copy to… group destinations by account and keep recent folders at the top. On desktop, Local Folders appears alongside your other accounts.

## Keep upcoming work beside your inbox

Checking whether you are free on Thursday shouldn’t pull you away from the message you are reading. On desktop, Calendar now lives alongside Tasks in the right-hand side panel.

Browse a compact month grid and a rolling 7-day agenda of events and due tasks beside your inbox or Kanban board. Expand to the full monthly calendar when you need to plan further ahead. Event and task reminders are available on desktop 0.4.8 and mobile 0.4.7.

<BlogImage src="/screenshots/blog/meron-048-calendar-panel.png" alt="Meron Kanban board alongside the new right-hand Calendar side panel showing a mini month grid and 7-day agenda" caption="The Calendar side panel docks next to your Kanban board or mailbox with a mini month view and a rolling 7-day agenda of events and due tasks." width={1280} height={820} />

- **Configurable event alerts:** Set default reminder times for timed and all-day events in Settings, or customize up to two alerts per event.
- **Task due times:** Set an optional time next to a task’s due date to get a reminder when it is due. Tasks with only a date remind you at 9:00 a.m. local time.

On desktop, page through a long message with Space or Shift+Space, then move to the next conversation without reaching for the mouse.
