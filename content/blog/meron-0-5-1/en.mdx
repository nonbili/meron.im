<PostSection>

Meron 0.5.1 gives you a few more ways to handle mail without breaking your flow: check unread messages from the macOS Dock, sign an email even when you can't encrypt it, and leave your usual sign-off out of a short reply.

<PlatformNote>The Dock badge is a macOS feature. Signature controls and the dark theme refresh are desktop improvements; notification action settings apply to Android. The three new languages are available on desktop and mobile.</PlatformNote>

</PostSection>

<PostSection>

<Badge icon="AppWindow">macOS</Badge>

## Check unread mail from the Dock

Meron's macOS Dock icon now shows your inbox unread count. You can see whether mail needs your attention while working in another app.

The macOS app icon also fits the Dock's icon grid more closely, and the window controls have more space around them.

## Sign a message without encrypting it

A contact doesn't need an encryption key for you to send them a signed message. Meron can now attach an OpenPGP signature to unencrypted mail, letting a compatible mail client check the signature while the message remains readable without decryption.

Open **Settings**, select your sending account under **Mail accounts**, and find its **Encrypted email** section. Once you've created or imported a key and enabled it, turn on **Sign unencrypted mail**.

## Leave the sign-off out of a short reply

Your email sign-off now has its own switch in quick reply and the full composer. Turn it off for a short note or back on for a more formal message, without editing the signature you've saved in Settings.

In quick reply, the saved signature stays out of the input box. The switch controls whether Meron adds it when you send. In the full composer, switching it off removes the signature from the draft, as shown below.

<BlogImage
  src="/screenshots/blog/meron-051-signature-toggle.png"
  alt="Meron composer with Elena's saved sign-off removed and the signature switch showing No signature. Click to add it; the message still contains a clickable URL"
  caption="The sign-off is removed from this draft; the saved template is still available from the switch."
  width={870}
  height={625}
/>

This is separate from the OpenPGP signature: one is your written sign-off, the other is a cryptographic signature.

Bare web addresses also become clickable links in sent quick replies, the full composer, and the reader. The `https://meron.im` link in these drafts started as a plain URL.

</PostSection>

## More depth in dark themes

Desktop dark themes now use layered surfaces and soft shadows to separate cards, menus, and panels. Opening a composer beside a Kanban board also keeps the board from shifting sideways, and the reader drops its frosted band.

When new mail arrives in a thread you're reading, Meron keeps the current message open so you can finish it.

</PostSection>

<PostSection>

<Badge icon="BellRing">Android &amp; Languages</Badge>

## Choose your notification actions

Android new-mail notifications gain a **Delete** button. Under **Settings → Notifications**, choose which action buttons appear so you can handle incoming mail from the notification tray.

Mobile light themes keep mail rows white and use bold text to distinguish unread messages. Contacts also show the correspondent's or account's profile avatar when available.

Meron is now available in **Dutch**, **Ukrainian**, and **Indonesian**, with missing translations filled in across existing languages.
